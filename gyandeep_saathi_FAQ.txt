─────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IS RTE 12(1)(c) / GYANDEEP
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Section 12(1)(c) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009 requires private unaided non-minority schools to reserve at least 25% of seats at the entry level for children belonging to:
- Economically Weaker Sections (EWS)
- Disadvantaged Groups (as notified by the State — e.g. SC/ST and other notified categories)

Admission under this quota is free of cost to the child up to Class 8.
The government reimburses private schools per-child as per rules (generally the lower of the school's fee or the state's per-child expenditure in government schools).

Minority-run unaided schools are exempt (as per the legal position noted in the product brief / Supreme Court judgment on Article 30(1)).

In Bihar, online registration and admission under this provision are done through the Gyandeep Portal.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
ELIGIBILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────

A child can typically be considered if:
- The admission is for the notified entry class (primarily Class 1 under Bihar's current Gyandeep implementation framework).
- The child belongs to a Disadvantaged Group OR Economically Weaker Section as defined by Bihar notifications.
- Parent/legal guardian completes online registration and application on Gyandeep with required verification.

Disadvantaged Group (as described in department flyer language):
- Includes notified categories such as Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, Backward Class, Extremely Backward Class (including transgender as notified), and minority groups where parent/legal guardian annual income is within the state-notified limit for that group.
- Exact income ceilings must be read from the latest official flyer/circular on Gyandeep.

Economically Weaker Section (EWS):
- Children of all castes/communities whose parent/legal guardian annual income is below the state-notified weaker-section income limit.
- Confirm the latest rupee threshold on the portal/flyer before quoting a number in production.

Orphan children:
- Means children whose parents are not alive.
- Special document rules apply (see DOCUMENTS REQUIRED).
- Caste certificate is not mandatory for orphans (as per flyer notes).
- Income certificate is not applicable to orphans (as per flyer notes).
- Parent Aadhaar is not required in orphan cases as noted on the flyer; guardian/institution head undertaking is used.

Age:
- Child must meet the age criteria notified for entry (as printed on the current Gyandeep admission notice). Always confirm the exact birth-date window on the latest circular.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
WHO IS NOT COVERED / COMMON LIMITS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Minority unaided schools are exempt from the 25% 12(1)(c) reservation.
- Process described here is for Gyandeep RTE private-school entry admissions — not a general scholarship DBT scheme.
- Paper applications are not the channel for Gyandeep online admission; use the portal.
- Quoting outdated income limits or deadlines without checking the portal is not allowed for the bot.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
APPLICATION PROCESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Applications are submitted online through:
- Gyandeep Portal: https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/

High-level steps (from department flyer):
1. Do preliminary registration of the child on Gyandeep using the parent/guardian mobile number.
2. At application time, parent/guardian Aadhaar verification is mandatory; child's Aadhaar verification is optional.
3. After registration, User ID is sent by SMS to the registered mobile number.
4. Login with the User ID and complete the online admission form on Gyandeep.
5. Track verification, school allotment and enrollment status on the portal.

Overall pipeline:
- Student registration
- Verification of registered students
- Online school allotment (lottery-based)
- Enrollment of selected students in the allotted school

Private schools with recognition also update Intake Capacity on the portal as per the notified schedule.

Current student registration last date (from flyer used for this demo pack): 31 January 2026.
Check Gyandeep regularly for extensions or changes.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Parents/guardians may need (as per flyer checklist):
- Birth proof: Birth certificate / hospital or nurse record / Anganwadi record / age declaration by parent or guardian
- Caste certificate (not mandatory for orphan children)
- Income certificate (not applicable to orphan children)
- Domicile Certificate OR alternatives such as Aadhaar / Voter ID / Driving Licence / Rent agreement / Electricity bill
  - For orphans, domicile rules differ as per flyer; if domicile is not available, parent/guardian must upload domicile via school within three months of admission where required
- Transgender-related certificate where applicable
- For orphans: Undertaking certificate by guardian / head of institution
- Parent/guardian Aadhaar (child Aadhaar after admission — see AADHAAR REQUIREMENTS)
- Parent/guardian mobile number
- Child's recent colour passport-size photo (about 50 KB as noted on flyer)

Always prefer the latest checklist on Gyandeep if it differs from this pack.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AADHAAR REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Parent/guardian Aadhaar verification is mandatory while applying.
- Child's Aadhaar is NOT mandatory at the time of application.
- After the child is admitted to school, parent/guardian must submit the child's Aadhaar to the school within three months.
- The headmaster uploads / causes upload of the child's Aadhaar on the Gyandeep Portal.
- For orphan cases, parent Aadhaar is not required as noted on the flyer; follow orphan document rules instead.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SCHOOL ALLOTMENT AND ENROLLMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- After verification, eligible students are considered for online school allotment.
- Allotment is lottery-based (as per Bihar Gyandeep process description).
- Selected students must complete enrollment/admission at the allotted school within the notified window, with required documents.
- If not allotted, check the portal for any further rounds, waitlist or next-cycle instructions from the department.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
OBJECTIONS / DOCUMENT ISSUES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

If verification finds a problem (missing/incorrect documents, data mismatch):
- Status may show documents pending / objection / needs correction (wording may vary on portal).
- Parent/guardian should correct and re-upload as instructed on Gyandeep or via the school.
- After correction, wait for re-verification.

If the school refuses enrollment despite allotment:
- Note school name, date of visit and what was said.
- Re-check allotment letter/status on portal.
- Register a grievance in Gyandeep Saathi and/or contact the helpdesk email / local education office as appropriate.

COMMON QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Question: Which website should I use?
Answer: https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/

Question: Is this free?
Answer: Admission under 12(1)(c) is free to the eligible child up to Class 8 as per the Act; schools are reimbursed by government as per rules.

Question: Can I apply offline on paper only?
Answer: Gyandeep admission is an online portal process. Follow the portal steps. For help if the portal fails, use help email / grievance.

Question: I did not get SMS User ID.
Answer: Confirm the mobile number used at registration, wait briefly, retry registration/login options on portal, or raise a Portal/login grievance in this bot and email rtebiharhelp@gmail.com.

Question: Child has no Aadhaar yet.
Answer: Child Aadhaar is not mandatory at application time, but must be submitted to the school within three months after admission for upload on Gyandeep.

Question: We are EWS but not SC/ST.
Answer: EWS is based on income criteria for all communities as notified. Confirm current income limit on the official flyer/portal.

Question: School says minority institution so no 25% seat.
Answer: Minority unaided schools are exempt from 12(1)(c). Verify the school's category through official information.
