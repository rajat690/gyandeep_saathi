─────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IS RTE 12(1)(c) / GYANDEEP
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Section 12(1)(c) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009
requires private unaided non-minority schools to reserve at least 25% of seats at the entry level
for children belonging to Economically Weaker Sections (EWS / Weaker Section) and Disadvantaged Groups.

Admission under this quota is free of cost to the child up to Class 8.
The government reimburses private schools per-child as per rules.

Minority-run unaided schools are exempt (legal position noted in product brief / Article 30(1)).

In Bihar, online registration and admission under this provision are done through the Gyandeep Portal.
Portal FAQ: admissions under 12(1)(c) are conducted through an online application process.

Q1. How are admissions done under RTE 12(1)(c)?
A. Admissions under Section 12(1)(c) of the Right to Education Act, 2009, are conducted through an online application process.

Q2. Where can I apply for admission?
A. Applications for admission are submitted online through the Gyandeep Portal.

Q3. What is the application process?
A.
- Register on the Gyandeep Portal
- Receive the USER ID via SMS on your registered mobile number
- Log in using the USER ID and Password and fill out the application form
- Parent/Guardian Aadhaar is mandatory
- Child's Aadhaar is not mandatory at the time of application but must be submitted within 3 months of admission

Q4. How do I select schools?  [WAS MISSING / WEAK IN OLD BOT PACK]
A.
- After filling out the application, you can select schools
- All schools within your block will be displayed
- You can select up to 5 nearby schools
- Priority is given based on distance:
  - 0–1 km: First priority
  - 1–3 km: Second priority
  - 3–6 km: Third priority
- Warning: Providing incorrect information may lead to rejection of the application.

Q5. How is seat allotment done?  [WAS MISSING DETAIL]
A.
- School allotment is done through an online lottery system
- 5% seats are reserved for Children With Special Needs (CWSN)
- Preference is given to children residing closer to the school
- The process is conducted at the district level

Q6. What is the verification process before admission?  [WAS MISSING]
A.
- After allotment, parents must submit documents to the allotted school
- The school verifies documents and distance
- Final verification is done by the Block Education Officer (BEO)
- Admission is confirmed only after successful verification

Q7. On what grounds can an application be rejected?  [WAS MISSING]
A.
- Incorrect or incomplete documents
- Not meeting eligibility criteria
- Duplicate applications

Q8. What is the role of the District Education Officer (DEO)?  [WAS MISSING]
A.
- Conducts the online lottery process
- Monitors the application and verification status
- Oversees implementation at the district level

Q9. What are the responsibilities of schools?  [WAS MISSING]
A.
- Admission of verified students
- Update student details on the portal
- Cannot deny admission to allotted students
- Update vacant seats on the portal
- Ensure no discrimination against admitted students

Q10. Who are included in the Disadvantaged Group?
A. Children belonging to SC, ST, OBC, EBC, and Minority communities whose parents/guardians
have an annual income up to ₹1,00,000.
(Portal FAQ figure — re-verify if circular changes.)

Q11. Who are included in the Weaker Section?
A. Children from all categories whose parents/guardians have an annual income up to ₹2,00,000.
(Portal FAQ figure — re-verify if circular changes.)

Q12. What is the age criteria for admission?  [WAS VAGUE IN OLD PACK]
A.
- Minimum age: 6 years
- Maximum age: 7 years 11 months 29 days
(As published on portal FAQ — confirm against current admission notice for the session.)

Q13. What documents are required for admission?
A.
- Birth Certificate
- Caste Certificate
- Income Certificate
- Residence Proof (Domicile / Aadhaar / Voter ID / Electricity Bill, etc.)
- Parent/Guardian Aadhaar Card
- Child's Aadhaar Card (to be submitted within 3 months of admission)
- Mobile Number
- Passport-size photograph

ELIGIBILITY (ALIGNED TO PORTAL FAQ + FLYER)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

A child can typically be considered if:
- Age meets portal criteria (min 6 years; max 7 years 11 months 29 days as per portal FAQ).
- Entry class is the notified entry class (primarily Class 1 under Bihar Gyandeep framework — confirm session notice).
- Child belongs to Disadvantaged Group OR Weaker Section as defined on portal/circulars.
- Parent/legal guardian completes online registration and application on Gyandeep with required verification.

Disadvantaged Group (portal FAQ):
- SC, ST, OBC, EBC, and Minority communities
- Parent/guardian annual income up to ₹1,00,000 (portal FAQ)

Weaker Section (portal FAQ):
- All categories
- Parent/guardian annual income up to ₹2,00,000 (portal FAQ)

CWSN:
- 5% seats reserved for Children With Special Needs in the allotment process (portal FAQ).

Orphan children (flyer notes — still apply where portal FAQ is silent):
- Means children whose parents are not alive.
- Caste certificate not mandatory for orphans (flyer).
- Income certificate not applicable to orphans (flyer).
- Parent Aadhaar not required in orphan cases as noted on flyer; guardian/institution head undertaking is used.
- If domicile missing, upload via school within three months of admission where required (flyer).

─────────────────────────────────────────────
WHO IS NOT COVERED / COMMON LIMITS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Minority unaided schools are exempt from the 25% 12(1)(c) reservation.
- Gyandeep is for RTE private-school entry admissions — not a general scholarship DBT scheme.
- Paper-only applications are not the Gyandeep channel; use the portal.
- Incorrect distance/school information can cause rejection.
- Duplicate applications can be rejected.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
APPLICATION PROCESS (COMBINED)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Portal: https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/

Steps:
1. Preliminary registration on Gyandeep with parent/guardian mobile number.
2. Parent/guardian Aadhaar verification mandatory; child Aadhaar optional at apply time.
3. USER ID sent by SMS to registered mobile.
4. Login with USER ID and password; complete online admission form.
5. Select up to 5 schools within the block (distance priority bands apply).
6. Track verification, lottery allotment, and enrollment on the portal.
7. If allotted: submit documents at school → school verifies docs/distance → BEO final verification → admission confirmed.

Pipeline:
Student registration → Verification → Online school allotment (lottery, district level) → Enrollment at allotted school.

Private schools with recognition also update intake / vacant seats on the portal as per schedule.

AADHAAR REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Child's Aadhaar is NOT mandatory at application time.
- After admission, parent/guardian must submit child's Aadhaar to the school within three months.
- Headmaster uploads / causes upload on Gyandeep.
- Orphan cases: follow flyer orphan Aadhaar/document rules.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SCHOOL ALLOTMENT, VERIFICATION, ENROLLMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Online lottery at district level (DEO conducts lottery).
- Closer residence gets preference; CWSN 5% reservation.
- After allotment: documents to school → school checks docs + distance → BEO final check.
- Schools cannot deny admission to allotted verified students; must update portal; no discrimination.
- If not allotted: check portal for further rounds / next instructions; do not invent waitlist rules.
- If school refuses despite allotment: note school name and date; re-check portal; register grievance in bot and/or email rtebiharhelp@gmail.com / local education office.

COMMON QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Question: Which website should I use?
Answer: https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/

Question: Where is the official FAQ?
Answer: https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/faq

Question: How do I see schools in my area?
Answer: Open https://gyandeep-rte.bihar.gov.in/registration-school-details , select District then Block, and review the school list.

Question: How many schools can I choose?
Answer: Up to 5 nearby schools within your block, with distance priority bands 0–1 km, 1–3 km, 3–6 km.

Question: Is this free?
Answer: Admission under 12(1)(c) is free to the eligible child up to Class 8 as per the Act; schools are reimbursed by government as per rules.

Question: What is the income limit?
Answer: As per portal FAQ: Disadvantaged Group up to ₹1,00,000; Weaker Section up to ₹2,00,000. Re-check live FAQ/circular before relying on the figure.

Question: What is the age limit?
Answer: As per portal FAQ: minimum 6 years; maximum 7 years 11 months 29 days. Confirm on current admission notice.

Question: Is there reservation for children with special needs?
Answer: Yes. Portal FAQ states 5% seats are reserved for CWSN in the allotment process.

Question: Can the school refuse an allotted child?
Answer: Portal FAQ says schools cannot deny admission to allotted students and must not discriminate; if refused, document the visit and use grievance / help email / local education office.

Question: I did not get SMS User ID.
Answer: Confirm mobile used at registration, wait briefly, retry portal options, or raise Portal/login grievance and email rtebiharhelp@gmail.com.

Question: Child has no Aadhaar yet.
Answer: Not mandatory at application; must be submitted to school within 3 months after admission for upload on Gyandeep.

Question: School says minority institution so no 25% seat.
Answer: Minority unaided schools are exempt from 12(1)(c). Verify school category via official information / school list fields where shown.
